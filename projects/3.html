Case study

Booktopia (2022) - solo designer

Intro

Booktopia is an app that helps people easily find and save books. In addition to the app, there is also a website.
**Personal project

Tools/software used: Adobe XD, Illustrator, Procreate

Process - Timeline, Mockups and Feedback

By browsing competitors I was able to see what worked and what didn’t. I took some inspiration from justwatch.com which isn't in the same industry but was acheiving a similar goal of consolidating multiple sites, movies, series, etc. took notes of the positives and what would make for a better user experience. I was able to build out a user journey based on my notes. Then I began working on wireframes

- What is the main goal of this website/app?
- Who is the target audience?
- Which experience should be prioritized? App or Website?
- How do we expect people to use this product?
- What colours would work best tp bring this to life?

After deciding on what part of the layout worked best, I made a high fidelity mockup and chose the colour palette. Initial sketches were done in Procreate. Final designs were completed in Adobe XD. Icons were created on Adobe Illustrator
