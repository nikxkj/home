My approach to UI/UX design I draw sometimes

Be it a revamp or a completely new project, I tend to follow the same steps: research, documentations, drafts, and design reviews.

A completely new project usually means a new brand guideline and new direction. In cases like that, there’s a lot of initial spitballing between the stakeholders and me. I’m able to build a direction based on the demographic for said product and any other consumer data that is provided. From there, I research competitors to note what the growing trend is and if that’s something that can be implemented to the project. Eventually, multiple images are sourced and different colour schemes are worked through. From there I work on mockups and build out the prototypes. My favourite tool is Adobe XD, although I also use Figma, depending on if the stakeholders/customer has a preference.

Flowcharts are also an important part of the process. It helps ensure that the user experience flows properly and that all the pages contained in the site/app can easily be navigated to.

Consistency with fonts, icons, CTAs is another important aspect of a site or an app's branding. I utilize the features on the various design tools (Adobe XD, Figma, Sketch) to ensure that I'm using consistent fonts styling and colours throughout. I always work out the colour palette and overall theme branding before I start a high fidelity design. Although most times, I provide multiple mockups where I use the colours in different ways

For a simple revamp, I’m usually working with existing brand guidelines and with a tighter timeframe. I’m quicker with this. Multiple drafts aren’t usually required. Although I always make sure to provide two hi-fi mockups. After some feedback and remarks from the stakeholders, I narrow it down to one design and refine as needed.
